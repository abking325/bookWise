BookWise — polished homepage + personal book tools
=================================================

OPENING IN TREBEDIT (ANDROID)
1. Extract the BookWise-Polished-Website ZIP.
2. Open the BookWise folder in TrebEdit.
3. Open index.html, then choose Preview/Run.

WHAT CHANGED
- Refined home hero with animated 3D book spines, realistic artwork, smooth hover/arrow/swipe navigation.
- The carousel uses the real book covers shipped in your original website.
- Pure-white background on Home and the site's original Manrope, Bodoni Moda, Literata and Ethiopic fonts.
- New Add Book control (plus) in the top bar.
- New Settings control in the top bar.
- "Books you add" collection section at the bottom of Home.

WHAT HAS NOT CHANGED
- The original React bundle (js/app.js) and main design stylesheet (css/style.css).
- Existing Home sections below the hero, Discover, Learn, Track, Access and reader.
- Original bilingual content, book catalog, progress tracking, and demo payment screens.

PERSONAL BOOKS
- Use Add Book to upload a cover and enter title, author, category, year and description.
- Optionally attach a PDF that you can open in your browser's PDF viewer.
- New books appear in the homepage hero and "Books you add" section.
- Personal books are stored LOCALLY in the browser's IndexedDB. They will NOT automatically sync between devices.
- Uploaded custom books do not gain chapter-based reading in the original reader automatically.
- Do not clear browser site data without backing up your personal materials.

SETTINGS
- Cinematic smooth / slow & gentle / no-animation mode
- Hover-to-select controls
- Display subtitles and natural shadows
- Saved locally on this device

ADDITIONAL FILES
  js/bookwise-catalog.js     - mapping to the 12 original books/covers
  js/bookwise-polish.js      - new home hero, settings, local book management
  css/bookwise-polish.css    - custom styles layered over the original site

The original BookWise source assets remain untouched.
